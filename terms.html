03 Nature of the Protocol

GRAVE is decentralized, autonomous, and immutable software. The GraveVault contract has no admin key, no upgrade path, and no pause switch. Once deployed, it executes exactly as written, and neither GRAVE nor anyone else can alter, reverse, freeze, or override its behavior.

The Interface is merely one convenient way to interact with the Protocol; the Protocol exists independently of it and can be used directly. We do not operate the blockchain, control the network, or process transactions on your behalf.

04 Non-custodial

GRAVE never takes custody of your assets or your keys. Assets you deposit reside in your own vault contract and remain under your control while your vault is active. You alone are responsible for your wallet, your private keys, and every transaction you sign.

We cannot recover lost keys, reverse transactions, cancel an execution, or retrieve assets sent in error. Onchain actions are final.

06 Your responsibilities

  • Keep your wallet and keys secure; you bear all consequences of their loss or compromise.
  • Verify all vault parameters — schedule, beneficiaries, shares (in basis points, summing to 10,000), and guardians — before you submit them.
  • Perform your proof-of-life check-ins on the schedule you set. Missed check-ins beyond the grace period will allow execution.
  • Verify beneficiary addresses. Assets sent to a wrong or inaccessible address cannot be recovered.
  • Confirm the transfer semantics of any token you deposit (fees, blocklists, pausability).
  • Ensure your use complies with all laws and regulations that apply to you.
