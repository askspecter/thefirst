Deploy the GRAVE Factory.

Deploy the protocol's factory contract to Robinhood Chain from your own funded wallet. You sign the transaction and pay gas in ETH — no private keys are ever shared. Deploy once; every vault is created through it.

Already deployed

A factory address is already configured/saved: . Deploying again creates a separate factory.

1 · Connect wallet

Connect the wallet you want to deploy from. It needs a little ETH on the target network for gas.

2 · Switch network

Your wallet is on the wrong network. Switch to the target chain to continue.

3 · Activate your address

Robinhood Chain rejects contract creation from an address it has never seen. Send one tiny transaction to yourself first (0 ETH, gas only) so the chain recognizes your address — required once per wallet.

4 · Deploy factory

This deploys GraveFactory. Confirm the transaction in your wallet and pay the gas. If it reverts with a new address, activate it first (step 3).
