How It Works

Four steps to an immortal will.

A GRAVE will is created once, verified continuously, and executed automatically — without anyone needing to lift a finger.

01

Create Your Will

Define beneficiaries, asset allocations, and release conditions directly onchain through the GRAVE dApp. No paperwork, no notary.

02

Lock Your Assets

Deposit crypto assets into your own non-custodial GRAVE Vault, deployed on Robinhood Chain. You retain full control at all times.

03

Proof-of-Life Check-ins

The Heartbeat Oracle periodically confirms you're active. Miss your check-in window, and a public grace-period countdown begins.

04

Automatic Execution

Once the grace period lapses, the vault permissionlessly and irreversibly distributes assets to your beneficiaries. Final. Forever.

Architecture

Built to outlive its creator.

Every layer of GRAVE is designed around one principle: no single entity — including GRAVE itself — should ever be able to stop your will from executing.

Immutable Vaults

Non-upgradable Solidity smart contracts hold each will independently. No admin key, no backdoor, no pause switch.

Heartbeat Oracle

Decentralized proof-of-life network verifying check-ins on a schedule you define, with multi-signal fallback to prevent false triggers.

Guardian Network

Optional trusted guardians can co-sign to pause a suspicious trigger — a social safety net without custodial risk.

Robinhood Chain Native

Deployed on an Arbitrum-Orbit L2 built for real-world assets — fast finality, deep liquidity, and fees low enough for global inheritance.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does GRAVE ever take custody of my assets?

Never. Assets stay in a vault contract you control until — and only until — your predefined conditions are met onchain. GRAVE has no admin key and cannot move funds on its own.

What happens if I simply forget to check in?

You'll get advance reminders before your check-in window closes, followed by a public grace-period countdown. Nothing executes instantly — there's always time to respond.

Is a GRAVE will legally binding?

GRAVE is a technical execution layer, not a law firm. It's designed to complement — not replace — your legal will. Always consult local regulations for your jurisdiction.

Why build on Robinhood Chain specifically?

Robinhood Chain's Arbitrum-Orbit architecture pairs low fees and fast finality with a growing base of tokenized real-world assets — a natural home for a protocol securing real generational wealth.

What if my beneficiaries lose access to their wallet?

The Guardian Network supports social-recovery flows for beneficiary wallets, reducing the risk of assets becoming permanently inaccessible after execution.
